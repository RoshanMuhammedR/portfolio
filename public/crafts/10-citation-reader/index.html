Why did ingestion fall behind during the upload spike?

The spike queued far more PDF parsing jobs than the workers could drain, and each worker handled one task at a time, so large files held small ones behind them.

Splitting parsing and embedding onto separate queues kept embeddings flowing while PDFs were still being parsed, which the runbook now lists as the first thing to check.
